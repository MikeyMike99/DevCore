4. Your IP address

Like every website, our hosting and security provider Cloudflare sees your IP address when you connect. Siraugga itself does not use your IP address to identify you, and does not store it.

The only use: so that clearing cookies can't give unlimited free prompts, guest prompts are also counted per network per day, with a generous allowance. For this, your IP address is turned into a scrambled code using a secret key that exists only in the server's memory and is replaced every day, so the code can't be turned back into your IP address or linked to you on another day. The counts are kept in memory only, never written to disk or logs, and disappear at the end of the day.

5. What you send us

  • Chat messages and files you upload are sent to AI model providers (such as Anthropic's Claude and Google's Gemini) or to a model on our own server, to produce the answer. Files you upload to the exam content folder are deleted after 30 days.
  • Tickets: the title, description, your answers to the ticket questions and your email address. An AI reviews the ticket, the admin team gets it by email, and if it's approved an AI agent may make the change on the website. Your email address is used only to tell you what happens to your ticket.
  • Sign-in details, if you create an account, are used to sign you in.

We keep server logs to run and secure the service. We don't sell your information or use it for advertising.

6. Voice dictation

The microphone button in the chat box lets you speak instead of typing. Your voice is turned into text entirely on your own device, by a speech-recognition model that runs inside your browser.

  • Your audio never leaves your device. It is not recorded to disk, not stored, and not sent to Siraugga, Google, Microsoft, Hugging Face or anyone else. It is discarded as soon as the text is ready.
  • Only the text is used, and only once you send it, exactly as if you had typed it (see section 5). You can read and edit it before sending.
  • The first time you use it, your browser downloads the speech model (about 40 MB, or about 145 MB on devices that use the graphics card for speed) from Siraugga's own server and keeps it in its cache. No third party is contacted.
  • Your browser asks for permission before the microphone is used, and the microphone is only on while the button shows Stop. You can withdraw the permission at any time in your browser settings.
