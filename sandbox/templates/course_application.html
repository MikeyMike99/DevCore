Step 1 of 6 • Welcome

Welcome. Take your time.

This is a hands-on, step-by-step course where you learn by doing, in a private practice space (a sandbox) set up just for you. You cannot break anything, and you can stop and come back whenever you like.

What happens when you press Start

  1. You read how your data is used, and agree.
  2. You register with your name and email.
  3. We email you a link. Opening it confirms it is really you and starts the course.

Nothing is asked of you on this page. All you need is a web browser; every tool is already set up for you.

Step 2 of 6 • Agreement

Your data and privacy, in plain words

What we use
Your name and email, your organization or project name, the commands and code you type in the labs, your predictions and reflections, your survey answers, and whether the optional screen recording is on or off.
What we use it for
Only to run your labs, build your evidence record, confirm your email address, and issue and email your completion certificate.
Where it is kept
In your own private sandbox on our server, separate from every other learner. If you turn on pair mode, the shared workspace is encrypted in your browser first. A screen recording stays in your browser until you download it and is never uploaded to a third party. Your signed certificate is kept in restricted storage and emailed to you.
How it is protected
An encrypted HTTPS connection, a private locked-down sandbox for each learner, secure cookies, and certificates digitally signed with Siraugga's cryptographic key.

What happens next: after you agree, the next card asks for your name and email. Then we email you a link to confirm it is really you.
